Windows creates a suspended process, establishes its kill-on-close Job ownership before execution, then resumes it. No ordinary Popen fallback or PID-tree taskkill is used. Close terminates its Job even when the leader previously exited, requires zero active Job processes plus leader wait, closes pipes/handles and joins IO under one shared 1.5 s resource budget. Original reset 2 s, action/server deadlines and protocol validation remain unchanged. This is a trusted local plugin lifecycle, not a malicious-code sandbox.
